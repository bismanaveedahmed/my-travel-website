TRAVEL TIPS

General Travel Tips

Before You Go

  • Research the destination: Know the weather, culture, and key attractions.
  • Pack light but smart: Include warm layers, sunscreen, power bank, reusable water bottle, and basic meds.
  • Backup essentials: Keep copies of your CNIC/passport, hotel info, and emergency contacts.

On the Road

  • Keep cash: Remote areas may not accept cards or have ATMs.
  • Start early: Roads in northern areas can be long and affected by landslides or weather.
  • Local SIM card: Use Telenor or SCOM for better coverage in GB and AJK.

In the Mountains

  • Acclimate slowly: Especially in high-altitude places like Fairy Meadows, Skardu, or Khunjerab.
  • Respect nature: Don’t litter, avoid disturbing wildlife, and stick to trails.
  • Stay hydrated: The cool weather can fool you, but dehydration is common at altitude

Cultural Etiquette

  • Dress modestly: Especially in small towns or conservative areas.
  • Be respectful: Ask before photographing people, and always greet with a smile or “Salam”.
  • Support local: Buy handmade crafts, eat local food, and hire local guides when possible.

Extra Pro Tips

  • Offline maps: Download Google Maps offline or use Maps.me for navigation without signal.
  • Portable charger: Power cuts are common in remote areas.
  • Weather check: Always check forecasts; snow or rain can delay plans.
